| 45 | Post-R5 / liquidity | House seed becomes ADAPTIVE: the house tops a table up to a per-room liquidity floor and withdraws to a token seed as real handle arrives (`core/liquidity.ts`), instead of staking a flat 50.00 per zone. | A flat seed is not liquidity, it is an averaging filter: the pari-mutuel mean is pinned at `(1−r)/(K−1) ≈ +17.6%` whatever we do, so the only lever is DISPERSION, and a dominant uniform seed destroys it. | 46 | Post-R5 / liquidity | The handle EMA is ASYMMETRIC — α = 0.25 rising, 0.6 falling. | math-model §6's low-population honesty note says a lone player against thin seeds faces a worse-than-nominal edge, and that a bigger `h` shrinks it. A symmetric EMA would leave the seed low for several rounds after a busy room emptied — exactly the window where a newly-solo player needs it high. Reacting fast to a room going quiet is player protection, not tuning. |
| 47 | Post-R5 / liquidity | The whale cap (B5) is evaluated against `max(liveHandle, liquidityFloorMinor)`. | Without the floor the adaptive seed turns B5 against the FIRST bettor: on a busy table the seed is a token 1.00, so the earliest anchor of a round would be capped at a quarter of six credits.
