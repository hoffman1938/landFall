The interval resamples ROUNDS, not bets. Landfall is pari-mutuel, so returns
inside a round are strongly dependent — one player's salvage is funded by
another player's lost stake. A per-bet interval would treat those as
independent and report a band several times too narrow.

READ THIS TABLE AGAINST THE OPERATOR NET ABOVE. Profiles differ in RTP because
crowd position redistributes expected value BETWEEN players — standing where
the crowd is not pays relatively more. What it never does is move value away
from the house: the operator's take is fixed at r/K of handle by the
pari-mutuel identity, whatever shape the crowd takes. That is the honest form
of the disclosure GLI-19 §4.6.1(a) requires for a game with a skill layer.
